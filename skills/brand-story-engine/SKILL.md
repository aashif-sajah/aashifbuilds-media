# Brand story engine

For Aashif (LinkedIn) and the ScaleDev co-founder (Instagram). A brand is the same few true stories and beliefs, told again and again, so people remember one thing about you.

## 1. Brand core (fill once, reuse forever)
Read /mnt/project-files/brand/brand-core.md if it exists. If not, build it with the founder by asking, one question at a time:
1. Who you help, in one line (default: founder-led UAE service businesses that lose enquiries on phone and WhatsApp).
2. The one thing you want to be known for (default: "the person who stops UAE businesses losing enquiries").
3. Three beliefs you'd argue for, each with a reason from your own experience.
4. Your origin story: where you started, the moment it turned, where you are now. Real facts only.
5. Five signature stories: a win, a mistake, a money moment, a "this is why I do it" moment, a funny one.
6. Proof you actually have today (zero is fine; say zero).
7. Words you use, words you'd never use.
Save it. Every later answer must fit it.

## 2. Positioning lines
Write 3 options for each: profile bio (Instagram 150 chars, LinkedIn headline 220), one-line intro for DMs, and a 30-second "what I do" spoken version. Specific beats clever: name the customer, the problem and the result.

## 3. Story structure for any post or reel
Pick one:
- Moment > tension > turn > lesson: open in the scene ("9:04pm, the clinic phone rang"), show what was at stake, what changed, what you took from it.
- Before > after > bridge: how it was, how it is now, the step in between.
- Mistake > cost > fix.
- Belief > story that proves it > what the reader can do.
Rules: one story per piece; start in the middle of the action; include one detail only the founder could know (a time, a place, a number, a quote); end on the lesson or a question from experience, not a slogan.

## 4. On-brand check (run on every draft)
Score yes or no: fits the brand core; one story or idea; a real detail; sounds like the founder talking; no invented clients, numbers or results; no guru phrases ("game-changer", "unlock", "secret"), no em dashes, no "it's not X, it's Y", no lists of three for rhythm. Fix every "no" before handing it over.

## Never
Invent stories, clients, results or quotes. Name a person or business without their OK. Describe Aashif as the sole founder.
