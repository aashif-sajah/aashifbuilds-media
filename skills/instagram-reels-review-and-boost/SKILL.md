# Instagram reels: weekly review and boosting

Claude reads the numbers and recommends. A founder presses Boost and pays. Claude never creates, edits or funds an ad.

## 1. Get the numbers, in this order

1. A connector with Instagram data in this chat (Metricool or Windsor.ai, both have free plans). Pull every reel from the last 30 days.
2. An export: Meta Business Suite > Insights > Content > Export, or the Instagram app's Professional dashboard. Look in /mnt/project-files/instagram/exports/ or the chat.
3. Screenshots of each reel's Insights screen.

If none exist, say what is missing and stop. Never estimate a number.

## 2. Log every reel

Keep /mnt/project-files/instagram/reel-metrics.md (create it on first use). One row per reel, read at 48 to 72 hours and again at 7 days:

| Date | Job | Hook type | Topic | Length s | Audio | Views | Reach | Non-followers % | Avg watch s | Watch % | Skip rate | Likes | Comments | Sends | Saves | Follows | Profile visits | Boosted | Notes |

- Watch % = average watch time ÷ length.
- Per 1,000 reach: sends, saves, likes, follows.
- Leave a cell empty when Instagram doesn't show the number. Organic and boosted numbers never share a row; boosts go in boost-log.md.

Benchmarks are the account's own medians. Industry averages stop mattering after 10 reels.

## 3. Weekly review (15 minutes)

1. Update the log and the medians of the last 10 reels.
2. Winners: top 20% on sends per 1,000 reach, with watch % at or above median. Losers: bottom 20% on watch %.
3. Each winner: what did the first 3 seconds do differently (hook type, frame 1, text, topic)? Plan 3 variations.
4. Each loser: where did people leave? Fix that beat in the next script.
5. Audience: non-followers %, and the UAE share of followers. We want a UAE-heavy audience, not just a big one.
6. Progress to the 1M target: the best reel's views this week and the trend.
7. Report in 5 lines: winner, loser, one change for next week, boost pick (or none), one question for the founders.

Don't judge a reel before 48 hours.

## 4. Should we boost this reel?

Boost only when all are true:
- It is 48 to 72 hours old and beats the account median on watch % and sends per 1,000 reach.
- Audio is original or from Meta Sound Collection (licensed music can make a reel ineligible).
- 9:16, a professional account, and not cross-posted to Facebook (cross-posted reels can't be boosted from Instagram).
- The likely viewers include UAE owners: a Trust or Ask reel, or a Reach reel on a UAE business topic.

Never boost to rescue a weak reel. Never buy cheap views from countries we don't sell in to reach a view count: they book no audits and teach the algorithm the wrong audience.

## 5. How to boost

Two routes (Enrich Labs and LeadEnforce, 2026):
- Boost button (Instagram app or Business Suite): the fastest way to amplify an organic winner. Goals offered: more profile visits, more website visits, more messages. Always pick the goal yourself; automatic choices tend to buy cheap engagement.
- Ads Manager: when the aim is leads, or when testing several reels against each other.

Goal by aim:
- Grow the UAE audience: Boost > More profile visits.
- Start audit conversations: Boost > More messages, with a ready-made first message such as "Free 15-minute audit", or Ads Manager > Engagement > Messages (Instagram).
- Website visits: rarely. For a founder brand, DMs convert better.

Audience: people living in the UAE (start with Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah), ages 25 to 60 as our default. Advantage+ audience widens beyond what you set and amplifies existing signals, so a clean UAE location matters more than piles of interests. If adding detailed targeting, keep it to 3 to 6 interests (small business, entrepreneurship, the segment's topic).

Budget ladder (our test rule, not a benchmark):
- Test one reel at AED 25 to 40 a day for 3 days. Test two reels only as two separate boosts.
- Judge at 48 to 72 hours. Keep it running only if cost per profile visit or per conversation beats the last best.
- Scale 10 to 20% every 48 to 72 hours while results hold (Enrich Labs). Stop after two checks in a row of rising cost.
- The founders set the monthly cap. Every boost goes in the log.

Creative: Meta's ad system leans on the creative to find people, so several different winners beat one reel boosted forever. Refresh when the same people have seen it about 3 times or results fall.

## 6. Read the results

Log to /mnt/project-files/instagram/boost-log.md:

| Date | Reel | Goal | Audience | AED/day | Days | Spend | Reach | Views | Cost per 1,000 views | Profile visits | Cost per visit | Follows | Cost per follow | Conversations | Cost per conversation | Audits booked | Decision |

- Cost per 1,000 views = spend ÷ views × 1,000.
- What counts, in order: audits booked, conversations, UAE follows, profile visits. Lots of boosted views with no visits or DMs is a weak result (LeadEnforce: high reel views often come with weak conversion).

## 7. Monthly

Did boosts raise the UAE share of followers? Did DMs turn into audits? Recommend next month's cap and the 2 hook types to keep. Show the founders before anything changes.

## Never

- Spend money, create or edit ads, or press Boost. Draft the exact settings; a founder does it.
- Promise views, follows or leads from a budget.
- Estimate a missing number.
- Use ad results as client proof in public.
