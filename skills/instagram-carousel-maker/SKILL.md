# Instagram carousel maker

Carousels earn saves and sends and give a second chance at reach: Instagram can show an unswiped carousel again from slide 2. Treat each one as a tiny story.

## 1. Plan
- One promise per carousel, stated on slide 1. Job: Reach (broad), Trust (UAE owner proof) or Ask (free 15-minute GCC Sales Pipeline Audit).
- 6 to 10 slides (Instagram allows up to 20). Fewer, stronger slides beat filler.
- Ask: who would save this, who would send it, and why?

## 2. Slide map
1. Hook slide: 4 to 9 words, big, one clear image or number. Makes them swipe.
2. Stakes: why this matters, with a real number or moment.
3 to 8. One point per slide, 15 to 30 words, a headline plus one line. Steps, checklist, before/after or a story beat by beat.
Second-last: the payoff or summary they'll screenshot.
Last: CTA matched to the job (save, send to a clinic owner friend, follow, or "DM AUDIT").

## 3. Copy rules
Headline first, detail second. Plain words, numbers as digits. Each slide must make sense alone and pull to the next ("the third one is the one nobody checks"). Caption: line 1 restates the hook with a search phrase (e.g. "WhatsApp automation UAE"), then 2 to 5 short lines, up to 5 hashtags. No em dashes, no guru phrases, no invented numbers.

## 4. Design
- 1080x1350 (4:5) every slide, same layout grid, big type, safe margins of 80px.
- Use the brand system in /mnt/project-files/brand/ (BRAND-VISUALS.md, templates/, fonts): Ink #111418, Paper #F3EEE5, Signal #FF5A1F for the one accent, Inter Tight headlines, Instrument Serif italic for one accent word.
- Adapt an existing carousel template in brand/templates/ (carousel, carousel-timeline, carousel-checklist, carousel-split, carousel-numbers); don't repeat the last design used (check linkedin-content/carousel-log.md).
- Render: cd /mnt/project-files/brand && node render.mjs templates/FILE.html OUTDIR. Images saved in project files carry a content-credentials tag; post from the aashifbuilds-media repo copy.
- Slide-number marker and handle on every slide. No stock photos, robots, gradients or fake screenshots; demos say "demo".

## 5. Output
For each slide: number, headline, body line, visual note. Then the caption, hashtags, alt text, and the rendered file paths.

## Never
Invent clients, results or screenshots. Quote prices.
