# Content pillar planner

Plans what to make; other skills write it (instagram-reels-engine, instagram-carousel-maker, brand-story-engine, aashifbuilds-linkedin-engine).

## 1. Discuss first
Aashif agrees topics and angles before anything is written. Present options, recommend one, wait for his yes.

## 2. Pillars (3 to 4, from the brand core)
Default set, change with the founders:
- Journey: building ScaleDev from zero, student founder, Sri Lanka to the UAE, real numbers (zeros stay zeros).
- Enquiry leaks: how UAE clinics, real estate and agencies lose leads on phone and WhatsApp, and what it costs.
- Builds and demos: AI receptionist, WhatsApp flows, CRM pipelines shown working (labelled demo until real clients exist).
- Opinions: beliefs about service businesses, AI and response time, each backed by a story.
Mix per week: about 60% reach (broad), 30% trust (UAE owner niche), 10% ask (free 15-minute GCC Sales Pipeline Audit).

## 3. Series beat one-offs
Turn pillars into named, repeatable series (e.g. "Missed call of the week", "Week N of building ScaleDev", "Would you automate this?"). A series makes planning fast and teaches the audience what to expect.

## 4. Weekly plan output
Table: day | platform | format (reel, carousel, LinkedIn post) | pillar | series | working title | hook type | job (reach/trust/ask). Instagram: 3 to 5 reels plus 1 to 2 carousels. LinkedIn: follow the approved plan (Mon broad story, Tue ICP, Wed student-founder journey, Thu build log). Re-use one idea across formats: a reel becomes a carousel and a LinkedIn post.

## 5. Use the numbers
Read the latest reel review (instagram/reel-metrics.md) and LinkedIn reviews before planning. Double down on the formats and hook types that beat the account median; drop the bottom ones.

## Never
Plan content around invented clients or results. Plan Automotech or CV content.
