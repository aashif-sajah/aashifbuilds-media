# Instagram Reels engine

Built for the ScaleDev co-founder's Instagram personal brand (reels only), and for Aashif's reels. Two goals at once: reels that travel to non-followers (target: 1M views on the best ones) and reels that bring UAE service-business owners to the free 15-minute GCC Sales Pipeline Audit. A view only counts if it can become a follower, a conversation or proof.

Research behind this skill (7 Nov 2026): Mosseri and Meta statements, the Instagram Help Center and vendor studies. Official facts are stated as facts; vendor numbers are marked. The account's own Insights overrule everything here after 10 reels.

## 0. Who is on camera

Read /mnt/project-files/instagram/creator-profile.md if it exists. If not, ask once and save the answers there (or keep them in the chat when there is no project folder):
1. First name, handle, city, languages they can speak on camera.
2. Their role at ScaleDev and a normal working day.
3. Three true stories they are happy to tell (a win, a mistake, a number).
4. What they can film: face to camera, screen recordings, office, car, street, events.
5. Style: talking head, voiceover over B-roll, or skits.
6. Topics, words or people that are off limits.

Never invent a name, story, client or number. ScaleDev has 0 published clients until a real one is logged in the project.

## 1. Give each reel one job

Plan each week as a mix:
- Reach, about 60%: broad stories anyone can feel or would send. The founder journey (student, Sri Lanka to the UAE), money and time, Dubai business life, an AI demo with a "wait, what?" moment, an opinion with a reason.
- Trust, about 30%: niche proof for UAE clinic, real estate and agency owners. Where an enquiry gets lost, what an unanswered 9pm call costs, a live build or demo, a before and after of a workflow.
- Ask, about 10%: one clear invite to the free 15-minute GCC Sales Pipeline Audit, with a DM keyword. No prices, ever.

The million-view reels will come from Reach. Clients come from Trust and Ask. Don't chase views with content no UAE owner would ever send to a friend.

## 2. What Instagram rewards (confirmed)

- Top three signals (Adam Mosseri, Jan 2025): watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach. Sends count slightly more for reaching non-followers, likes for followers. Comments are not on the list.
- Meta's Q3 2026 earnings call: Reels now rank sequences of reels using "richer watch time signals". Holding attention is the whole game.
- Originality is enforced: an account that reposts 10+ pieces it didn't make in 30 days drops out of recommendations; videos with a visible watermark (TikTok, CapCut) aren't recommended; adding only a border, subtitles or a credit doesn't make something original. Since 30 Apr 2026 this covers photos and carousels too.
- Unlabeled AI-generated people can lose recommendation eligibility (Aug 2026). No AI avatar or cloned voice standing in for a founder.
- Reels longer than 3 minutes aren't recommended to new audiences (Instagram Help Center).
- Maximum 5 hashtags per post since Dec 2025. Hashtags label a reel; they don't create reach.
- Captions are used for discovery, and public professional accounts' posts can appear in Google (since Jul 2025). Keywords in the caption and spoken in the reel matter more than hashtags.
- Treat claims like "hook in 1.7 seconds" or "a send is worth 5 likes" as vendor opinion.

## 3. The hook (first 1 to 3 seconds)

Three layers, one message:
- Visual: a face or movement in frame 1. No logo, intro card or "hey guys".
- On-screen text: 3 to 8 words, readable in one second, clear of the bottom fifth and the right edge where the buttons sit.
- Spoken: the first sentence states the payoff or the tension.

Hook types for this niche (tag every reel with one):
- number: "This missed call cost a Dubai clinic AED [real figure]."
- scene: "9:04pm. The clinic is closed. The phone rings."
- demo-first: the AI receptionist picks up on screen; explain afterwards.
- result-first: only with a real, logged result.
- journey: "Final-year student. Running an AI company for UAE businesses."
- opinion: "Most UAE clinics don't need a new website." Then the reason.
- mistake: "We lost our first lead because of this."
- POV skit: "POV: you call a Dubai agency at 6:05pm."
- comparison: "Same enquiry. Two businesses. One replies in 9 seconds."

Write 3 hooks per reel. Film the best; the other two are Trial Reel tests.

## 4. The body: keep them watching

- One idea per reel. Two ideas make two reels.
- Change something on screen every 2 to 4 seconds: cut, punch-in, angle, B-roll, screen recording, text.
- Re-hook at the midpoint with a reveal or a new question.
- Cut breaths, filler and repeats. Say the number, never "a lot".
- Burned-in captions on every reel.
- Length by job: one-liners and loops 7 to 15 s; stories and demos 20 to 45 s; deep value 45 to 90 s only if the script still pulls when read aloud. Never over 3 minutes.
- End on the payoff line or loop back into frame 1. No "thanks for watching", no outro card.

## 5. Make it sendable and saveable

Before writing, answer: who would send this, to whom, and why? No answer, new angle.
- Sendable: a cost people didn't know, a moment every owner has lived, a funny but true scene, a demo that looks impossible.
- Saveable: a checklist, steps, numbers worth copying.
- Ask for the send ("send this to a friend who runs a clinic") at most once a week.

## 6. Caption copy

- Line 1 shows before "more": the hook in new words plus one search phrase ("AI receptionist Dubai", "WhatsApp automation UAE", "missed calls clinic").
- Then 2 to 5 short lines: the context or the detail the reel couldn't fit.
- CTA by job. Reach: send it, or follow for part 2. Trust: save it, or ask a question in the comments. Ask: "DM AUDIT for the free 15-minute GCC Sales Pipeline Audit."
- Up to 5 hashtags: one niche, one topic, one place (for example #dubaibusiness #aiautomation #dubai). Tag the location on Trust and Ask reels.
- Voice: honest, specific, a smart operator, never a guru. Banned: "game-changer", "unlock", "level up", "secret", em dashes, "it's not X, it's Y", tidy lists of three, questions you answer yourself.

## 7. Output format (per reel)

REEL n: working title | Job: Reach, Trust or Ask | Hook type | Target length
HOOK: visual / on-screen text / spoken, plus 2 alternates for Trial Reels
SCRIPT: timestamped beats (0:00-0:03 ...) with [B-ROLL], [SCREEN], [CUT] notes
SHOT LIST: numbered, filmable on a phone, with framing
ON-SCREEN TEXT: per beat
AUDIO: original voice, Meta Sound Collection or trending track, and whether it can be boosted
COVER: frame plus a 3 to 5 word title (the profile grid crops to 3:4)
CAPTION: full text
HASHTAGS: up to 5
POSTING: day, time window, Trial Reel yes or no, Story share, comment to pin
CHECK AT 48 H: the one number that says whether it worked

## 8. Score before filming

Score each 0, 1 or 2. Film at 14 or more out of 20; otherwise rewrite.
1. Frame 1 has a face or movement.
2. Text hook readable in one second.
3. Spoken hook states the payoff or tension.
4. One idea only.
5. A visual change every 2 to 4 seconds.
6. A clear "who sends this to whom".
7. A specific number, place or moment.
8. Original footage, no watermark.
9. Length fits the job and is under 3 minutes.
10. The CTA matches the job.

## 9. Posting

- Professional account, Creator type: Creator accounts get the wider music library; Business accounts get a limited one.
- Trending audio has to be added in the Instagram app (schedulers can't attach licensed tracks). A reel you may boost should use original audio or Meta Sound Collection: licensed music can make it ineligible for boosting.
- Test hooks with Trial Reels (shown to non-followers first, results in about 24 hours, can auto-share to followers if it does well within 72 hours). Some sources say the feature needs 1,000+ followers.
- 3 to 5 reels a week, every week. Vendor data (Buffer, 2.1M posts) found 3 to 5 posts a week more than doubled follower growth compared with 1 to 2.
- Timing: start Monday to Thursday, then follow Insights "Most active times". Test 2 or 3 slots; ignore "best time" lists.
- After posting: share to Story, reply to every comment the same day, pin the best one.
- Don't delete and re-upload a flop. Re-cut it with a new hook and post it as a new reel a week later.
- The best reels can go to LinkedIn as native vertical video with a rewritten caption.

## 10. Next batch

After 48 to 72 hours, run instagram-reels-review-and-boost (weekly review and boost picks). When a reel beats the account median on sends per reach, make 3 variations: new hook, new example, part 2.

## Never

- Invent clients, results, numbers, screenshots or testimonials. Demos say "demo" and use test data.
- Name a prospect or client without their written OK.
- Use someone else's video, or download from TikTok with the watermark on.
- Use an AI avatar or cloned voice as the founder.
- Sign off with Aashif's "peace." unless the creator chose it.
